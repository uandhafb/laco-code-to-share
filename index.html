      how this site works

      The sound is Strudel, a JavaScript port of TidalCycles. The visuals are Hydra, a video synth by Olivia Jack. Both run in your browser, and the code is right here in the panel for you to edit.

      Wrap layers in drums(…) or melody(…) to hook them to the floating controls. In Hydra, pulse follows the drums and pointer.x / pointer.y follow your mouse.

      Inspired by strudel-pie by Gabriel Vigliensoni.
